RISC-V Machine Lab

The RISC-V emulator comes from Takahiro's riscv-rust under the MIT License.
https://github.com/takahirox/riscv-rust/tree/8ee69d7a5dc7ef6d8b2bda96bf86d2923f2cf176
https://github.com/CC-David-CC/riscv-rust/tree/webos/browser-files/webos

The xv6 kernel and filesystem are the exact binary fixtures distributed
with that riscv-rust source. The upstream build notes and binary update
are recorded in the emulator repository.
https://github.com/takahirox/riscv-rust/tree/8ee69d7a5dc7ef6d8b2bda96bf86d2923f2cf176/resources/xv6
https://github.com/takahirox/riscv-rust/commit/341e8f481ee5511e7372e7e9822aa88671f6569c
